About Us

Why people keep coming back.

Musafir Cafe began in 2019 as a tiny room in Manali with a few shelves of old books, one chess board and a single coffee machine. We had one simple idea: every traveller deserves a quiet place to pause. Over the years, the shelves grew, the guest book filled up and strangers became regulars.

People come here to slow down. Some come for the warm chai and homemade food, some for a good book or a game of chess, cards or board games with new friends. And almost everyone leaves their best moment in our guest book, so the next traveller can read it and smile.
